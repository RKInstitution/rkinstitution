Percentage, written as a fraction

In this bank a multiplier is a fraction. 5/2 means five halves. It does not mean the ratio 5:2. The same question is printed in English and in Hindi.

Profit percent = (profit / cost price) × 100. If profit is 1/6 of the cost price, the percent is (1/6) × 100 = 50/3.

1. The cost price of an article is ₹200. It is sold at 5/2 of the cost price. The profit is:

किसी वस्तु का क्रय मूल्य ₹200 है। उसे क्रय मूल्य के 5/2 पर बेचा जाता है। लाभ है:

  1. ₹200
  2. ₹250
  3. ₹300
  4. ₹400

Answer (c). Selling price = 5/2 × 200 = 500. Profit = 500 − 200 = 300.

2. A watch was bought for ₹3,000 and sold for ₹3,500. Profit = 500/3000 = 1/6 of the cost price. The profit percent is:

एक घड़ी ₹3,000 में खरीदी गई और ₹3,500 में बेची गई। लाभ प्रतिशत है:

  1. 1/5
  2. 50/3 %
  3. 3/20
  4. 1/4

Answer (b). (1/6) × 100 = 50/3 %.

3. 1/5 of a number is 40. The number is:

एक संख्या का 1/5, 40 है। वह संख्या है:

  1. 80
  2. 160
  3. 200
  4. 400

Answer (c). Number = 40 × 5 = 200.

Police, Railway, SSC, and state papers all start from this habit: write the fraction, then the percent.
