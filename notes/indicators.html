Indicators

An indicator is a dye or substance that changes colour or odour in acidic and basic media. We use it so that nobody has to taste a chemical. Sour taste in lemon and curd comes from acids. Bitter taste and a soapy touch come from bases.

Hyperacidity after oily food is excess hydrochloric acid in the stomach. Lemon and vinegar add more acid. A mild base, baking soda solution (NaHCO₃), neutralises it.

Olfactory

An olfactory indicator changes its smell. Onion juice, vanilla essence, and clove oil keep their smell in acid. In a base the smell is lost. A visually impaired student can use that change.
