All-Exam Foundation · 2026–27

One foundation. Five doors.

Bihar Police, Railway, SSC CGL, SSC MTS, and BPSC share the same arithmetic, the same English, and the same NCERT facts. The foundation is the course. Each exam later adds only its own pattern.

Maths · 20 topics

Number to mixed revision

Number system is taught. Simplification through number series follows the same template: board rule, two examples, DPP.

English · 14 topics

Grammar to cloze

Parts of speech through cloze test. The quant book beside it already runs to 30 chapters, from percentage to data interpretation.

GS · NCERT only

95 days on the syllabus

History, civics, geography, economy, and Class 6–10 science. A removed NCERT chapter is not taught. History days H01 to H04 are written.

Reasoning · 12 topics

Analogy to seating

The reasoning list is fixed: analogy, series, coding, blood relation, direction, syllogism, calendar, and seating.
